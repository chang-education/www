Guitar Instrument

A guitar is a stringed instrument that is played with a pick or finger and usually has six strings. The sound of the guitar is produced by the vibration of the strings, and the sound of the vibrating strings is either acoustically amplified through a hollow enclosure (such as an acoustic guitar) or electrically amplified through an amplifier and speaker. This instrument is played with two hands. One hand hits the strings with a pick or with the tip of the finger so that the strings vibrate, and at the same time, the fingers of the other hand change the tuning of the strings by placing themselves behind the frets and changing the length of the strings. This is how musical notes are produced. Outside of the standard acoustic and electric guitars, there are many different types of guitars and sub-genres that have become popular in today’s music.
